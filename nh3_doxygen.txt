nh3.c Firmware Overview
=======================

This file lists the Doxygen comments generated from `nh3.c`, a simple
STM32 firmware demo for the HTS1510 pressure and temperature sensor. The
program polls the sensor, updates two 4–20 mA PWM outputs and logs data
over UART while refreshing the watchdog.

Main functions:
- `press_raw_to_psia()` – convert raw ADC counts to pressure.
- `temp_raw_to_C()` – convert raw ADC counts to temperature.
- `pwm_update()` – update the PWM channels with new values.
